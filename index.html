Frequently Asked Questions

How do we give student data and photos?

Register your school in the box at the top of abapps.in. Click Auto-Load to import your UDISE student list automatically, then use your phone camera to take photos!

Why are printed lanyards charged in batches of 30?

Digital print lanyards are sublimation-printed in rolls of 30 pieces. If your quantity is not a multiple of 30, our calculator shows you how adding a few extra cards gives you maximum value.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery takes 5–7 days across India after your lanyard design and student verification PDF are confirmed. Flat ₹150 delivery charge applies.
